Overview

Hazard records live in spreadsheets, shared drives and email threads. The golden thread is lost. Sentinel Log holds every hazard in one living register and links hazard, cause, consequence, control, evidence and residual risk end to end. An audit trail and end-to-end traceability stay with the record. It supports general hazard identification, design risk assessment, HAZOP and FMEA/FMECA.

FAQ

Who owns the data?

You do. The product runs under your control. Full export to xlsx, CSV and JSON with stable IDs keeps your records portable.

Where does it run?

On your infrastructure: Docker or Podman Compose with PostgreSQL, on Rocky, RHEL-compatible Linux or Ubuntu.

Can it work offline?

Yes. Designed for offline, air-gapped installation on isolated networks.

How do we get data in and out?

Import hazards, controls, evidence and actions from Excel or CSV; export the full dataset to xlsx, CSV and JSON; re-import your own JSON into an empty instance.

Can we tailor risk matrices and workflows?

Yes. Risk criteria, status labels, reference lists and related configuration are held as data and admin-managed after handover. You configure the product rather than fork it.

What happens if we stop working with you?

You keep the software under Apache-2.0, your data in open formats, and a customer-operated install you already control. No lock-in.
