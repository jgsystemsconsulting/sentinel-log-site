# Sentinel Log — treatment (dark-keynote adaptation)

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **Countdown to audit** — a compliance clock ticks through missing evidence; product appears as the last item. Rejected: implies a crisis event and invented pressure stats.
2. **Before / after split** — left pane = spreadsheet hell, right pane = register. Rejected: split-screen is the style cliché for SaaS; weak signature shot.
3. **Lost thread → snap → stitch → plan** — disorder as generated UI; one amber caret restores a grid; the golden thread stitches hazard→evidence; register and plan-on-a-page; end on the thread line. **Chosen:** matches the product verb (link), gives one native snap-grid move, and ends on the brand line without fake metrics.

## 2. Logline and arc

**Logline:** When hazard truth is scattered across files, an amber thread pulls every cause, control and evidence record into one living register — Sentinel Log.

**Arc:** Setup (scattered records, thread lost) → Turn (snap + stitch of the golden thread) → Ending (register, senior plan-on-a-page, quiet product truths, end card).

## 3. Benchmark (grammar only)

- Learn: black-stage product reveals (one line per screen, soft light, snap motion); process music that subtracts to a single note; insert cuts that land on grid.
- Do not take: any real brand UI, system chrome, melodies, fonts as marks, or competitor layouts.

## 6. Cue map (draft)

- BPM ~96 in disorder (detuned mallet crumbs); freeze at t=8.0 (digital zero).
- Turn bar: single centred amber “glass tap” family on snap landings.
- Thread section: additive vibraphone/marimba, one note per node light.
- Register/plan: sparse pad + low sine pulse; duck −8 dB under voice.
- End card: subtractive process down to one tone under the final line.
- Instruments: VCSL-class mallets/pads per STYLE.md; fetch at score stage.
