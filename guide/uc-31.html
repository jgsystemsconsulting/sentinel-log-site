UC-31: Audit trail

Main flow

  1. The administrator opens the audit trail at /hazards/audit/; the list renders with actor, action, and record columns (req-52).
  2. The administrator filters the trail by actor uat-admin; only that actor's entries remain (req-52).
  3. The administrator opens the configuration audit at /hazards/configuration/audit/; configuration-scoped entries render actor and action (req-52).
  4. The administrator opens one export entry; the detail names the actor, the action, and the exported object (req-52).

Not in release: Token issue, scoping, and revocation are not in this release.

Cited check phrases

  • filters trail actor uat-admin
