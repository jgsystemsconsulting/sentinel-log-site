Review and approve

  1. Viewer posts a comment on the sample hazard; comment renders with author and body. See Figure 1.
  2. Reviewer records a formal review on the sample hazard with outcome, comments, and required changes; review renders pending.
  3. Reviewer rejects that review with the reject decision; review renders rejected with required changes visible.
  4. Record carries the seeded approved review; approved outcome renders with reviewer and date.
  5. Hazard reviews tab groups reviews and comments with counts.
