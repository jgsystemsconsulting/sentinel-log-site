Guide

UC-9: Comment; review and approve. Actor: Reviewer

Main flow

  1. viewer posts a comment on UAT-0001; comment renders with author and body (req-24).
  2. reviewer records a formal review on UAT-0001 with outcome, comments, and required changes; review renders pending (req-25).
  3. reviewer rejects that review with the reject decision; review renders rejected with required changes visible (req-25).
  4. record carries the seeded approved review; approved outcome renders with reviewer and date (req-25).
  5. hazard reviews tab groups reviews and comments with counts (req-25, req-24).
