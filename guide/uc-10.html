Filter and sort the register

Guide

  1. Viewer opens the register with no query; the sample hazard renders and the count line states totals with archived excluded.
  2. Filter by status Open. The sample hazard stays listed and the applied filter chip renders.
  3. Search for the sample hazard; only the matching record lists and the count line states one shown.
  4. Viewer groups by residual risk; one band header per category states its hazard count.
  5. Viewer sorts residual score high first; the sort control shows the active rule.
  6. Every risk cell renders the colour rule chip with written category and icon, never colour alone.
