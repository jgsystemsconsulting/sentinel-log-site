Guide

UC-4: Copy hazard or control. Actor: Hazard owner

Main flow

  1. editor opens the UAT-0001 hazard detail page and submits the Copy form; the product creates a new hazard with a fresh identifier and the same editable fields, and the copy page names its source (req-6).
  2. editor opens the UCTL-0001 control detail page and submits the Copy form; the product creates a new control with a fresh identifier, creation route copied, and a link back to its source (req-6, req-12).
  3. editor opens the copy hazard causes tab and adds a cause; the cause renders under the copy with its own identifier (req-4).

Missing still: The frame set has no copy-dialog still. Do not treat another screen as the copy dialog.
