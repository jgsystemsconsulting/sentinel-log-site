Copy a hazard or a control

Guide

  1. Open the sample hazard detail page and submit the Copy form. Sentinel Log creates a new hazard with a fresh identifier and the same editable fields. The copy page names its source.
  2. Open the sample control detail page and submit the Copy form. Sentinel Log creates a new control with a fresh identifier, creation route Copied, and a link back to its source.
  3. Open the copy hazard causes tab and add a cause. The cause shows under the copy with its own identifier.
